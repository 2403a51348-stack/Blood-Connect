About BloodConnect

Connecting Compassionate Donors with Critical Patients

BloodConnect was created to eliminate the agonizing delay often experienced by families and emergency rooms searching for compatible blood donors in moments of trauma, pediatric surgery, cancer treatment, and emergency labor.

Traditional blood banks frequently face seasonal shortages of rare blood phenotypes (such as O- and AB-). BloodConnect provides an agile software-based coordination layer that helps patients discover voluntary donors in their immediate geographical vicinity.
