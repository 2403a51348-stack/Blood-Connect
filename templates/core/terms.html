Critical Medical & Non-Emergency Notice

BloodConnect is a web coordination system and NOT an emergency ambulance dispatch or licensed medical facility. If you are experiencing an acute, life-threatening trauma or medical emergency, immediately dial 112 or 108, or proceed directly to the nearest hospital trauma center.

1. Scope of the Platform

BloodConnect provides preliminary, software-based discovery to facilitate direct communication between voluntary donors and individuals requiring blood. We do not manufacture, store, test, sell, or transport blood components.

2. Medical Verification Requirement

The compatibility matching algorithm operates as an initial discovery filter based on standard red-cell ABO/Rh rules. Software matching does NOT substitute for mandatory laboratory cross-matching, antibody screening, disease testing, or pre-donation physical exams conducted by accredited blood bank personnel.

3. Prohibited Conduct & Commercial Prohibition

Blood donation through BloodConnect is strictly voluntary and non-remunerated. Offering or demanding financial payment, remuneration, or commercial compensation for blood donation is strictly illegal under the National Blood Policy and will result in immediate account termination and legal reporting.

4. Limitation of Liability

BloodConnect, its developers, and contributors shall not be held liable for any delays in donor arrival, clinical complications during or following transfusion, or donor cancellation.
