Architectural Overview

How BloodConnect Coordinates Life-Saving Matches

A deep dive into our software-based matching logic, clinical screening rules, and user workflows.

For Blood Donors

1
Create Verified Profile

Register with your blood group (A+, O-, etc.), city, and phone number. Your phone is kept private.

2
Control Availability

Toggle your status between Available and Unavailable with one click whenever you are traveling or recovering.

3
Receive In-App Alerts

When an emergency occurs nearby for a compatible patient, receive instant notifications with hospital location and urgency.

4
Accept or Decline

Accepting shares your contact info securely with the requester for direct hospital coordination.

For Requesters & Patients

1
Post Emergency Request

Submit patient name, hospital name, city, units required, and select urgency (Low, Medium, High, Critical).

2
Instant Ranked Matches

The algorithm evaluates registered donors and ranks them by compatibility percentage and proximity.

3
Broadcast or Target

Send direct requests to top-ranked available donors or broadcast to all eligible donors in your city.

4
Track Live Responses

Monitor responses in real time. As soon as a donor accepts, their phone number is revealed for swift arrival coordination.

The Donor Matching & Ranking Algorithm

Each potential match between an emergency Blood Request and a registered Donor is assigned an objective score out of 100 points based on 5 weighted criteria:

40 Points
Blood Group Compatibility

Exact blood group matches receive 40 pts. Clinically compatible red-cell donors (e.g. O- donating to A+) receive 30 pts.

25 Points
Donor Availability

Active donors currently marked as Available receive 25 pts. Unavailable donors receive 0 pts.

25 Points
Geographic Proximity

Donors situated in the identical city receive 25 pts; same state receives 15 pts; out-of-state receives 5 pts.

10 Points
Activity & Reliability

Past verified donations (up to 6 pts) and fresh availability verification within the last 7 to 30 days (up to 4 pts).

General Blood Donor Eligibility Criteria

  • Age between 18 and 65 years.
  • Body weight of at least 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • Hemoglobin level not less than 12.5 g/dL.
  • Minimum 90-day interval between whole blood donations.
  • Free from acute infection, fever, or antibiotic regimens.
  • Normal pulse (60-100 bpm) and blood pressure.
Preliminary Discovery Notice: The algorithm is strictly a preliminary locator for establishing rapid contact. Compatibility verification, clinical screening, infectious disease assays (HIV, Hepatitis B & C, Syphilis, Malaria), and antibody cross-matching must be conducted by qualified blood bank technicians before any transfusion.
