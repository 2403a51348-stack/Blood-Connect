Simple, Transparent Process

How BloodConnect Works

A structured three-step coordination workflow designed for speed and accountability.

1. Post Emergency Need

Requesters or hospitals submit patient details, required units, blood group, hospital location, and urgency status.

2. Intelligent Matching

Our algorithm evaluates red-cell compatibility, donor availability, distance, and activity score to rank eligible donors.

3. Rapid Coordination

Donors receive instant in-app alerts, accept requests, and coordinate with the hospital for medical cross-matching.

Clinical Foundations

Red Blood Cell Transfusion Compatibility

BloodConnect integrates standard transfusion compatibility rules into its preliminary algorithm:

  • O Negative (O-): Universal red cell donor. Can donate to all ABO/Rh blood groups.
  • AB Positive (AB+): Universal red cell recipient. Can receive red blood cells from all groups.
  • Rh Factor Rules: Rh-positive individuals can receive Rh-positive or Rh-negative blood, while Rh-negative individuals can generally only receive Rh-negative blood.
