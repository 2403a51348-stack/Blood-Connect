Privacy & Confidentiality

BloodConnect Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 | Last Updated: 2026

1. Commitment to Personal Data Protection

BloodConnect respects the sensitive nature of health and contact data. This policy outlines how we collect, store, and restrict access to donor and requester personal information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Donor Information: Full name, verified blood group, contact telephone number, residential area/city, availability state, and optional past donation records.
  • Requester Information: Contact person, telephone number, patient reference name, hospital facility, units required, and clinical urgency level.
  • System Telemetry: Log timestamps, notification delivery statuses, and session identifiers for security auditing.

3. Strict Disclosure Safeguards (Donor Privacy)

To prevent spam and protect donors, a donor's personal telephone number and full residential address are NEVER publicly searchable on open web search pages. Contact details are only revealed to a requester when a donor explicitly reviews and Accepts a specific blood donation request.

4. Password and Credential Security

All user passwords are encrypted using industry-standard salted PBKDF2 with SHA-256 password hashing. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

5. Account Deactivation & Data Deletion

Users retain the right to mark their profile as Unavailable or request total account removal at any time by contacting privacy@bloodconnect.org.
